Most founder events in Mumbai follow the same format. A big room, name tags, a few pitches, and a lot of conversations that end with "let's stay in touch."

10x Founders is the opposite on purpose. It's an invite-only group of founders, operators and leaders under 25, and it stays under 25 people.

## Three rules

**Invite-only.** Every person in the room was chosen. That isn't about exclusivity; it's about trust. When everyone knows the group is small and selected, people say what's actually hard about their company instead of what sounds good.

**Introductions with a reason.** I only introduce two people when there's a clear reason for them to meet. No blanket "you two should connect." If I can't say in one line why the meeting is worth their time, it doesn't happen.

**Small, in person.** We meet in places like Colaba and Alibaug, in groups small enough for one conversation. No stage, no pitch slots.
