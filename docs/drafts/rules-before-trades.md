I'm not SEBI registered, and nothing here is advice, a tip or a signal. This is how I run my own money, written down so you can see the process, not copy the positions.

The short version: I decide the risk before I decide the trade. Everything else follows from that.

## Equities: a valuation gap, then the work

For stocks I use a value-investing approach, in four steps.

1. **Look for a valuation gap.** I screen for businesses where the market price appears lower than the underlying value.
2. **Review the financials.** Balance sheet, cash flows, margins and debt, before I take any position.
3. **Check the advantage.** I look for something durable: pricing power, network effects, brand strength or lower costs.
4. **Adjust the position.** I add or reduce as the price moves, but the original risk limits stay in view.

## Crypto CFDs: the rules are written down

Alongside equities I trade crypto CFDs using several strategies. The mix changes with the market. The rules don't.

- Entries and exits follow written rules rather than impulse.
- I size every position in advance and cap the risk on each trade.
- I use leverage cautiously and account for the added risk.

## Why I share the process and not the picks

I teach a community of 1,500 people every day, and the most useful thing I can give them is a way of thinking that still works when I'm not in the room. A tip expires. A process doesn't.
