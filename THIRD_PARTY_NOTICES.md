# Third-party notices

The DarkAuroraBackground, FineGrainPattern, AnnotatedText, and TestimonialCard components are adapted from [Opensource UI](https://github.com/bidyut10/opensourceui), revision `d239c3463c984a099dd2cb67817a41fc0bcc876f`.

Local changes include a dark grain option, Next.js client boundaries, inherited text styles, and unique decoration IDs, and accessible testimonial controls with explicit data rather than sample endorsements.
